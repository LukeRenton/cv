I’m incredibly grateful to share that I won the Best Applied Project Award (2025) at the Wits Innovation Day, sponsored by
PYGIO
.
My honours research focused on a problem that sits at the intersection of AI, privacy, and digital heritage:
👉 How do we responsibly digitise historical documents without exposing sensitive personal information?
I developed a confidence-driven adaptive preprocessing pipeline that dynamically processes each document based on its unique degradation. This simple but powerful idea improved redaction accuracy by 28.5% compared to static, one-size-fits-all redaction pipelines.
This award means a lot, but it wouldn’t have been possible without the invaluable support and guidance of my supervisors, Dr
Steven James
and Dr
Benjamin Rosman
.
I’m excited about the real-world impact this approach can have, especially for privacy-preserving digitisation initiatives in South Africa and beyond.
